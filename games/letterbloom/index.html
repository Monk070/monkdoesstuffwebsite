How to Play Letter Bloom

🎯 Goal

Find all the target words by dragging letters to form words that fill the crossword grid.

✏️ How to Form Words

Click and drag across letters in the wheel to form words. Release to submit. Words must be at least 3 letters long.

🔗 Crossing Words

Words cross each other in the grid and share a letter where they meet, just like a classic crossword:

T
H
E
E
R

Here "THE" (across) and "HER" (down) share the letter H. The pink square belongs to both words.

💡 Tip: When you solve a word, its letters instantly appear in every word that crosses it. Use those revealed letters as clues when you're stuck!

💡 Hints

Stuck? Use a hint to reveal a random letter in the crossword. Each hint costs 5 points and you're limited to 3 hints per level.

⭐ Scoring

  • Target Words: 10 points per letter
  • Bonus Words: 5 points per letter (valid words not in the target list)
  • Level Complete: 50 points + (10 × level number)

🎮 Controls

  • Shuffle: Rearrange letters for a fresh perspective
  • Music: Use the media player below the crossword
  • Keyboard: Space to play/pause music, Ctrl+Arrow keys to change tracks
