HOOKLINE.fun

Interactive TikTok Live version

Your viewers guess the song from the tiniest clip, straight from the chat: leaderboards, the wheel, gifts and the points bank. Runs on your PC, connects to your TikTok Live - nothing to install for your viewers.

Download Hookline.zip

Windows · free · includes everything, even the runtime - nothing else to install

1 Get the game

1

Download Hookline.zip with the button above, then find it in your Downloads folder.

2

Right-click the zip and choose Cut.

3

Create a new folder somewhere sensible - Documents or Desktop both work nicely.

4

Paste the zip into your new folder, click it once to select it, then right-click and choose Extract All…

5

Open the extracted Hookline Packaged folder.

2 Launch it

6

Double-click HOOKLINE START.bat. The game opens in your browser.

Blocked by your security settings? No problem - use the alternative launch below.

Alternative launch (if Windows blocks the one-click file)
7

Click the address bar at the top of the folder window (the empty space to the right of the folder path).

8

Type cmd and press Enter - a terminal opens already pointed at the right folder.

9

Type hook…

10

…press Tab to autocomplete the full name, then press Enter. Same result, no security prompt.

3 Go live

11

Start your stream in TikTok Live Studio, then enter your username in Hookline with the @ in front and press Connect. That's it - you're connected to your viewers and their guesses count.

12

Adjust the game rules to taste - rounds, difficulty, genres, eras.

13

Adjust the gifts and bank settings if you want gifts and likes paying into the points bank.

14

Adjust the look and sound to fit your display - the layout is built for the 9:16 portrait ratio your viewers see.

15

Press F11 in your browser to go fullscreen. Happy streaming!

The song catalogue keeps growing. Keep an eye on the Discord for catalogue updates, and press Update Catalogue in the game to fetch the latest songs.
